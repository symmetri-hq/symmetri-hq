### Symmetri Engineering

Symmetri is a non-custodial technological swap currency platform and B2B closed-loop clearing engine.

**Security & Intellectual Property Notice:**
To protect our proprietary routing logic, AES-256-GCM encryption infrastructure, and blind indexing execution, all core backend (Python/FastAPI) and frontend (Next.js) repositories are maintained strictly as **Private**.

As a platform architecting integrations for enterprise retail POS systems and multi-source FX consensus engines (via OpenExchangeRates, OANDA, Chainlink, and Fixer), we do not expose our internal architecture, database schemas, or closed-loop digital voucher mechanics to the public domain.
